# Critical Lift Plan — LIFT-042

Project: Tower 4 structural steel package
Crane: Crane 4, tower crane, luffing jib
Lift reference: LIFT-042
Revision: 3
Prepared by: project engineering
Approved by: site lift supervisor (named approval required on the day of the lift)

## 1. Scope of the lift

Placement of a pre-assembled steel spandrel section onto the Floor 12 slab edge,
picked from the Zone B material staging area and landed within the Zone C lift
exclusion zone. The lift crosses the Zone A east corridor at height.

## 2. Environmental limits

Operations must stop when the measured wind gust exceeds the maximum of 42 km/h
at the jib head. This is a project-specific limit for this lift and it is lower
than the crane manufacturer's generic in-service limit because of the sail area
of the spandrel section.

A gust reading at or above the limit requires the load to be landed and the lift
suspended. The lift may not resume on a single lower reading; see section 6.

## 3. Load limits

The maximum planned load utilisation for this lift is 85% of the rated capacity
at the working radius. Utilisation above that figure invalidates this plan and
requires re-engineering, not operator judgement.

Hydraulic oil temperature must remain below 80 °C during continuous cycling.

## 4. Exclusion zone

Exclusion zone C must be kept clear of all personnel and material whenever the
lift is active. Zone C is barricaded at the Floor 12 slab edge and monitored by
the appointed banksman.

Entry into zone C during an active lift is a stop-work event. Material stored on
or across the zone C boundary is also a stop-work event, because it prevents the
banksman from confirming the zone is clear and it obstructs the landing area.

Zone A is the designated safe walking route while the lift is active. Personnel
displaced from zone C must use the zone A east corridor.

## 5. Data and instrumentation requirements

The lift supervisor must be able to verify current wind, load and zone-presence
data before and during the lift. Where a critical reading is stale, missing or
otherwise unverified, the lift must escalate to the site controller and remain
suspended until coverage is restored. An unverified condition is treated as an
adverse condition, not as an acceptable one.

Critical sources for this lift are the jib-head anemometer, the crane load
gateway and the Floor 12 zone-presence gateway.

## 6. Restart procedure after a stop-work event

The lift may only restart when all of the following are complete:

1. Zone C is confirmed clear by the banksman, visually and over the radio.
2. Wind gust has been re-checked and is below 42 km/h across a settled two
   minute observation, not a single instantaneous reading.
3. Load utilisation is confirmed at or below 85%.
4. All critical data sources are reporting fresh readings.
5. A named, qualified lift supervisor records the approval and the reason.

No automated system may issue this clearance. A named human approver is required
for the permit to be valid.

## 7. Conflicting information

Where a verbal report conflicts with instrumented or visual evidence, the more
restrictive interpretation applies until the conflict is resolved on site. A
verbal "zone clear" call does not override a visual obstruction.
