# Safety SOP — Crane and Suspended Load Operations

Document: SOP-CRN-014
Revision: 7
Owner: site safety

## 1. Purpose

Defines the mandatory controls for lifting operations with a suspended load,
including exclusion zone management, stop-work authority and the approval path for
resuming a suspended lift.

## 2. Stop-work authority

Any worker may call a stop-work. A stop-work call is never overridden by
production pressure. The following conditions are automatic stop-work events:

- a person inside an active exclusion zone;
- an environmental reading above the limit stated in the applicable lift plan;
- a load above the planned utilisation stated in the applicable lift plan;
- loss of communication with the appointed banksman.

## 3. Exclusion zone control

Exclusion zones are barricaded and signed before the lift begins. The appointed
banksman confirms the zone is clear immediately before each pick. Zone control is
a physical control; a radio call is a confirmation of that control, not a
substitute for it.

Where a worker enters an active exclusion zone, the load is landed or held and
the worker is directed to the nearest safe route. The worker must acknowledge the
instruction before the lift resumes.

## 4. Decision support systems

Monitoring and decision-support systems, including RiskTwin, are advisory. They
assemble evidence and recommend an action. They do not issue permits, they do not
control equipment and they do not replace the appointed banksman or the lift
supervisor.

An automated recommendation of HOLD or ESCALATE must be treated as a stop-work
call until a qualified person reviews it.

## 5. Data integrity

Safety decisions require verified current data. Where a critical monitoring
source is stale or unavailable, the operation escalates and the affected control
reverts to the manual procedure. Absence of data is not evidence of a safe
condition.

## 6. Approval to resume

Resuming a suspended lift requires a named, qualified approver, a recorded reason
and evidence that each triggering condition has cleared. The approval and its
evidence are retained for audit.

## 7. Privacy of monitoring evidence

Video evidence retained for safety review must have identities obscured before it
leaves the local processing workspace. Monitoring is limited to active safety
operations and must not be used for general workforce surveillance or for
identifying individuals.
